# Animated templates: recipes for explainer scenes (`"recipe": "<slug>"`)

Generated by `scripts/build.py --catalog` from the headers in templates/animated/. Do not edit by hand.

- **balance**: Balance scale · for: comparing two sides: costs vs benefits, pros and cons, which weighs more · params: left{label, value (or one per step)}, right{label, value (or one per step)}, unit?, decimals?
- **bars**: Bars · for: comparing amounts between a few items · params: items[{label, value, step?, color?}] (up to 7), unit?, decimals?, max?, highlight? (index that stands out)
- **board**: Departure board · for: many cases whose status changes: trials, flights, orders, applications, tests · params: title?, rows[{label, status (text, or one per step)}] (up to 8), colors? {status: accent|accent2|accent3}, status up to 12 characters, cols? (default: the longest status)
- **chat**: Chat · for: a conversation, a chatbot or assistant, customer service, how two parties exchange information · params: messages[{from: a|b, text, step?}] (up to 8; a = the contact, on the left; b = you, on the right), names?{a} (the contact's name, shown in the header and the chat list)
- **conveyor**: Conveyor · for: a process with stations: manufacturing, a request going through a system, a pipeline, a supply chain · params: stations[{label}] (2-5), item? (short text on the moving piece), at[]? (station index for each step; default: step 1 at station 0, step 2 at 1…)
- **document**: Document · for: contracts, laws, reports, forms, approvals and rejections, what a paper says · params: title?, lines? (number of text lines, default 10), highlights[{step, line, color?}], stamps[{step, text, color?}] (up to 3)
- **donut**: Donut · for: how a whole splits into parts: budget shares, market shares, composition · params: parts[{label, value, step?, color?}] (2-5), center? (text in the middle, or one per step), unit?
- **fill**: Container that fills · for: levels, capacity, progress, how full or empty something is · params: levels[] (0 to 1, one per step), shape? (tank|battery|column), values[]? (number shown per step; default: the level in %; give [] to show no number), unit?, labels[]? (caption per step), color?
- **funnel**: Funnel · for: how many make it through each stage: sales, admissions, selection processes, drop-off · params: stages[{label, value}] (3-5, from widest to narrowest), unit?, build? (default true: one stage per step)
- **grid100**: Grid of 100 · for: proportions: x out of 100, how many people, part of a whole · params: values[] (how many are lit at each step), total? (default 100), glyph? (persona|casa|moneda|gota|arbol|punto|cuadro), labels[]? (a caption per step), color?
- **line**: Trend line · for: how a figure changed over time, trends, before and after a moment · params: points[{label, value}] (2-24), until[]? (index of the last point drawn at each step; default: all at step 1), unit?, decimals?, min?
- **nodes**: Nodes · for: systems, networks, pods and servers, organizations, who talks to whom · params: nodes[{id, label, step?, color? (or one per step)}] (up to 10), links[{from, to, step?}], hub? (id in the center), flow? (true: dots travel along the links)
- **stack**: Stack of layers · for: things built in layers: a technology stack, a pyramid of needs, geological strata, foundations of an idea · params: layers[{label (short: up to ~40 characters, wraps in two lines), step?, color?}] (2-7, bottom first)
- **swarm**: Swarm · for: a crowd that sorts itself: many cases in categories, distributions, who belongs where · params: groups[{label, count, color?}] (2-4; up to 160 dots in total), phases[]? (one per step: mix | group; default: mix, then group)
- **terminal**: Terminal · for: commands, logs, code, what a computer does or answers · params: title?, frame? (mac: a macOS window, the default | terminal: a classic full-screen console), lines[text or {text, step?, color?}] (up to 12; step = when it appears, default 1; a line starting with "$ " is a command and gets a colored prompt)
