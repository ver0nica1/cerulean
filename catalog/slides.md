# Static templates: layouts for slides (`"slide": "<slug>"`)

Generated by `scripts/build.py --catalog` from the headers in templates/static/. Do not edit by hand.

- **big-number**: Big number · for: one figure that must land · params: value, unit?, caption (**highlights** allowed), kicker?, source?
- **bullets**: Bullets · for: a short list of points the speaker develops one by one · params: title, items[] (3-6 short phrases, **highlights** allowed), kicker?, build? (true: one item per step)
- **cards**: Cards · for: a few parallel ideas, options, features or examples · params: title, items[{title, text, tag?}] (2-4), kicker?, build? (true: one card per step)
- **comparison**: Comparison · for: two options, before and after, myth versus fact · params: title?, left{title, items[]}, right{title, items[]}, kicker?, build? (default true: left, then right)
- **image-text**: Image and text · for: a real screenshot, product, place or photo with its explanation · params: title, text? or items[]?, image{src, placeholder, fit? (fit: the whole image, the default | fill: fills the box, cropped), focus?{x, y} (with fill: the point that stays in view, in %)}, side? (left|right, where the image goes), kicker?
- **process**: Process · for: steps in order, a method, how something is made · params: title?, stages[{title, text?}] (3-5), kicker?, build? (default true: one stage per step)
- **quote**: Quote · for: a real quotation from a person or a source · params: quote (**highlights** allowed), author, role?
- **section**: Section · for: opening a new part of the talk · params: title, kicker?, text?, number?
- **statement**: Statement · for: one strong idea or conclusion said in a single sentence · params: text (with **highlights**), kicker?, source?
- **stats**: Stats · for: a few figures to compare at a glance · params: title?, items[{value, label}] (2-4), kicker?, source?
- **table**: Table · for: comparing several items on the same attributes · params: title?, columns[], rows[[...]] (up to 6 x 5), highlight? (row index from 0), kicker?, source?
- **timeline**: Timeline · for: history, milestones, how something evolved · params: title?, events[{date, title, text?}] (3-6), kicker?, build? (default true: one event per step)
